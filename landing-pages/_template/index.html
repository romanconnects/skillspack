Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know before you enroll.

What exactly is the 90-Day Financial Blueprint?
It's a structured 12-week coaching program where Eric & Mary walk you through building a budget, eliminating debt, and creating a savings system tailored to your income and goals.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you attend the sessions and don't feel it's right for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund.
How much time do I need each week?
Plan for about 2–3 hours per week: one live session plus light homework. All sessions are recorded, so you can catch up anytime.
What kind of results can I expect?
Results vary by starting point and effort, but most participants leave with a working budget, a debt-payoff plan, and their first funded savings goal within the 90 days.
Do I need any prior financial knowledge?
None at all. The program is designed for beginners and starts with the fundamentals before building toward advanced strategies.
