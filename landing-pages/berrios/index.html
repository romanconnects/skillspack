Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know before you restart.

What is the 90 Days Restart Program?
It's a 12-week coaching program where Antonio Berrios guides you through a complete financial reset — stabilizing cash flow, tackling debt, and building the habits that make your comeback permanent.
What is your refund policy?
You're covered by a 14-day money-back restart guarantee. Show up, do the work, and if it's not right for you within the first 14 days, email us for a full refund.
How much time do I need to commit each week?
Around 2–3 hours a week: one live session plus a short weekly action step. Every session is recorded, so life doesn't have to slow your restart down.
What results can I realistically expect?
Results depend on your starting point and effort, but most participants finish the 90 days with a working budget, a clear debt-payoff plan, and their first funded savings goal.
I feel like I'm starting from zero — is this for me?
Absolutely. The Restart Program is built for people rebuilding after a setback. Antonio starts with the fundamentals and meets you exactly where you are — no judgment, just a plan.
